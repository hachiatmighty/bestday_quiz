The method, in plain words.

A wheel from your answers.

Each slice combines four statements and the area’s one-word check. Statements about what’s working and what’s costing you count in opposite directions.

At least 3 answers are needed to draw an area. Fewer answers, or a skipped area, leave it missing.

There’s no overall life grade or comparison with other people. The questions were written for this audit. They have not been validated as a psychological scale.

Questions before you start.

Is the Life Audit free?

Yes. Your wheel, suggested focus and one area read appear first. First name and email open the full result, including the circle read and goal tools.

How long does it take?

About 8 minutes for about 52 answers in five parts. Most answers are a single tap. The goal at the end can be a suggestion or your own sentence.

Can I skip an area?

Yes. Skipped areas stay missing on your wheel. They’re never filled in as zero. An area needs at least 3 answers to appear.

Do I have to take the suggested focus?

No. It’s the lowest reading among the areas you said matter this quarter. The reason is shown, and you can choose a different area.

Is this a personality test?

No. It describes the last four weeks from your own answers. It doesn’t give you a personality type, diagnosis or prediction.

Will my circle see my answers?

Your answers are private by default. At the end, you choose who gets to see your goal. You can take stock before deciding to tell anyone.
