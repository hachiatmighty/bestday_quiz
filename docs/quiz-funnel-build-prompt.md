## 1. CONTEXT

You are building a top-of-funnel goal-archetype quiz for Bestday (bestday.ai), a co-achievement system. Bestday puts a circle around your goals: the people you respect, a coach, and an AI companion that remembers your progress, so you follow through, witnessed and held.

- Belief: **No one achieves alone.**
- Public platform line: **Do it. Together.**
- Member shorthand: **Your best circle.**
- Narrative thread (use with the Lagos, Accra, Nairobi and diaspora audience): **If you want to go far, go together.**
- Brand archetype: the Devoted Ruler. The auntie who remembers the goal you set six months ago, asks warmly and pointedly how it is going, celebrates loudly, and will not let you quietly give up on yourself.

Do not use legacy lines ("Where we achieve together.", "Real accountability. Real results.", "the coachieving app").

**Current state:** 7 paying members, roughly 36 active members, 87 Instagram followers, no paid budget. Growth so far comes from one-to-one outreach through three partner channels (coaches, communities, creators). They convert well but are capped by human negotiation time.

**Audience geography:** Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and the UK. WhatsApp is the distribution reality. Design every shareable artifact for a WhatsApp forward first.

**Audience psychology** (from the social skill, section 7). Segment by relationship to being held, not by country:
- **The Self-Made** ("I hold everyone. I don't need holding."): the core quiz-taker. Pride reads being held as weakness. The quiz must make being held read as strength and leadership.
- **The Seeker** ("I know what being held feels like. I just can't find it anywhere."): responds to calibre, discretion and cultural fluency. Fears fake community.
- **The Weary** ("Nothing works, including this."): should arrive through a forward from someone they trust, never cold. The share loop is their front door.

**The goal:** get Bestday in front of roughly 10,000 new people a day within 30 days through organic, self-propagating distribution. Treat this as a stretch target, not a forecast. Say what viral coefficient and seed volume it would take.

## 2. THE ASSET

A 10 to 12 question goal-archetype quiz. Under 90 seconds. Not a survey.

- A survey takes from the person. A quiz gives back.
- The payoff is an identity, not a score or report.
- The result card is the advertisement. Build it to be screenshotted and forwarded.
- Primary persuasion job: **Hearts** for the quiz and result, **Minds** for the bridge, **Wallets** only at the offer. Do not blur these.

## 3. VOICE RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Everything follows the Devoted Ruler tone: warm (never just soft), exacting (never just harsh), present (never just available), grounded (never just plain), rousing (never just loud).

1. **Seen clearly, not flattered blindly.** Results should feel specific enough to be personal and loose enough that nobody argues. The reader thinks "that's me." But the brand does not coddle: the result names a real standard the person holds themselves to.
2. **Lead with strength.** Every archetype opens with what this person is good at. For the Self-Made reader, that strength is often the fact that everyone leans on them.
3. **Tendencies, never verdicts.** A slip is a pattern that can show up, not an identity. "Your fuel can dip," never "you burn out."
4. **No false specificity.** "After a few weeks," never "at week three." Nothing fact-checkable against the reader's life.
5. **No clinical, diagnostic or therapy language.** No "personality type," "assessment," "trauma," "self-sabotage."
6. **Every result lands in the same place:** this person does their best work when they are not doing it alone. It must feel like recognition, not a sales turn. The brand-only test applies: if the line could sit on a habit tracker or gym app unchanged, rewrite it until a specific relationship changes the meaning.
7. **Language rules:** say "members" (never users or customers), "circle" only for real, identifiable people (never group, network or community in member-facing copy), "follow through" (never optimise, hack, crush). Sentence case. Headlines as questions or commands, per `voice-headlines.html`.
8. **Banned:** em-dashes (use a period, comma or line break), "unlock," "level up," "crush your goals," "stay motivated," "become your best self," "game-changer," "It's not just X, it's Y," autopilot tricolons, filler rhetorical questions, exclamation-mark enthusiasm, and every other item in the AI-writing tells list (social skill, section 6). Read every line aloud before shipping.

## 4. THE FIVE ARCHETYPES

Use these five. The names below are recommended updates to the original list (see note), and each is paired with a pictogram from the brand set.

| # | Name | Strength | Tendency | Pictogram |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Sprinter** | Moves fast, starts before ready, creates momentum others feel | After a few weeks, the fuel can dip | `03_willpower.png` |
| 2 | **The Planner** | Builds excellent systems, thinks in structure, prepares well | Energy can go into preparing rather than starting | `02_goal.png` |
| 3 | **The Anchor** (was Lone Wolf) | Self-reliant, resourceful, the one everyone else leans on | Can carry things alone longer than they need to | `11_isolation_community.png` |
| 4 | **The Explorer** (was Drifter) | Curious, high energy, open to possibility, moves easily between things | Direction can stay loose | `06_witnessed.png` |
| 5 | **The Finisher** | The rare closer. Follows through and ships | Can take on more than one finish line at a time | `05_success.png` |

Name note: "Lone Wolf" celebrates solo achievement, which is the exact belief Bestday exists to reverse (and the brand animal is the lioness, whose pride hunts together). "Drifter" reads as a verdict, and nobody forwards a verdict. The Anchor maps directly to the Self-Made posture. If you disagree, present the alternative with a recommendation.

For each archetype produce:
- Name
- One-line identity statement
- Strengths paragraph
- Soft tendency paragraph (a pattern, never a flaw)
- "What you need" section: how a circle, a coach and an AI companion that remembers would fit *this* type specifically. The Sprinter needs someone who notices when the pace drops. The Anchor needs to be held by someone for once. Make each bridge different.
- A short card line built for the shareable card
- A one-line WhatsApp caption the person would send with it

Structure every result as: human truth, then mechanism, then one low-friction invitation.

## 5. DELIVERABLES

### 5.1 Quiz questions
10 to 12 questions, each with 4 or 5 options that map to archetypes. Ask about real behaviour around goals ("It's the second week of a new goal. What's usually happening?"), not abstract self-description. Plain, globally readable English that works equally in Lagos, Accra, Nairobi and London. Avoid idioms that only land in one place. Faith, family, career, finance, wellness, relationships and play can appear as goal contexts, but never assume faith. Include scoring and mapping logic, tie-break rules, and which question should also capture the person's goal category (used to tint the card).

### 5.2 Result copy
All five archetypes, fully written, per section 4.

### 5.3 Result card design spec (and render the five cards)
Vertical, screenshot-native, legible as a WhatsApp thumbnail and as a Status or Reel background.

- **Sizes:** 1080 x 1920 (Status, Stories, Reels) as the master. Also 1080 x 1350 for the Instagram feed. Check that name and pictogram survive the small WhatsApp chat thumbnail crop.
- **Palette:** from `colors_and_type.css`. Bestday Yellow `#FFDE59`, deep black `#0D0D0D`, cream `#FFFCEF`. Solid backgrounds, no gradients. Recommend one: yellow card with black type (conviction, celebration) vs dark card with yellow type (authority, product-native). Optionally let the member's goal category accent (`--bd-cat-*`) appear once, as a small detail, never as the background.
- **Type:** Urbanist. Archetype name in 800 or 900 weight, large. Identity line in 700. Supporting lines in 500. Sentence case throughout.
- **Illustration:** the archetype's pictogram from the brand set, large and generous with negative space. Sparkles (`sparkles*.png`) are allowed once, because a result reveal is a celebration moment. Not as wallpaper. Tom the mascot only if it earns its place.
- **Hierarchy:** pictogram, "I'm The [Name]", identity line, one or two short result lines, then a quiet footer with the Bestday logo (`logoblacktrans.png` or `logowhitetrans.png`) and the quiz URL.
- **Rule:** the card should read as Bestday before the logo is seen (palette, Urbanist, pictogram style, Devoted Ruler line).
- Render all five cards as PNGs using the `bestday-carousel` skill's rendering pipeline where it helps, and include them with the spec.

### 5.4 Landing page copy
Pre-quiz entry page. Hook (question or command, sentence case), one-line promise, honest social proof, single call to action.

- **Social proof:** do not invent it and do not use the unverified "95%" statistic from the design system. With 7 paying members, social proof comes from named coaches and partners who agree to be quoted, or from a live "people who took it today" count once real. Flag what needs sign-off.
- **Performance:** assume a mid-range Android on mobile data. Specify a page budget, Urbanist subset to two weights with `font-display: swap` and a system fallback, pictograms as inline SVG or compressed PNG, no hero video.
- Visual treatment follows the product: dark surface `#0D0D0D`, yellow pill CTA with black text, 12px radius.

### 5.5 Email and WhatsApp capture
Where in the flow to capture and what the value exchange is. Test a teaser-then-capture pattern (show archetype name and pictogram, ask for contact to unlock the full result and the "what you need" section). Recommend email, WhatsApp number, or both for this audience, with the consent language and the reason. Keep the ask at CTA ladder level 4 or lower. No fake urgency.

### 5.6 The offer
What the person is invited into right after the result. Connect this archetype's specific need to a circle, a coach and the AI companion. Include CTA copy and a recommendation on trial and pricing framing.

**Product-claim gate:** before writing the offer, list which of the following are live today and mark anything unconfirmed as [G]: coach matching, AI companion memory, cohorts, referral rewards, circle invites. Only promise what exists. Do not say the AI "remembers everything."

### 5.7 Share mechanics (the growth surface)
- Share prompt copy on the result screen.
- A pre-written WhatsApp forward message (sounds like the member wrote it, first person, one line of personality, the link).
- A "find out your circle's types" loop: the member names or sends to the two or three people who'd tell them the truth. This is on-brand because the product *is* your circle. Use CTA ladder level 3 ("send to one trusted person").
- A circle view: when friends complete it, show how the types in one circle fit together. Say whether this is v1 or later.
- Any share incentive must be something that exists. If referral rewards are not live, recommend a non-monetary incentive (for example the circle view unlocks after two friends finish).

### 5.8 Distribution plan (30 days)
Channel posture comes from the social skill, section 9, and it partly conflicts with the original brief. Resolve it explicitly:

- **WhatsApp is primary.** Groups (alumni, estate, professional, faith groups where members already belong), Status, and one-to-one forwards. For faith groups, the quiz is shared by members, never used as a conversion device.
- **TikTok:** the toolkit marks it "drop" for the Self-Made and Seeker and "experiment for proof only" for the Weary. The original brief treats it as a primary surface. Present both options with a recommendation, and if TikTok stays in, frame it as a named experiment with a stop condition.
- **Instagram:** required for the Self-Made. Archetype-reveal Reels and carousels built in the brand system.
- **LinkedIn:** required for the Self-Made and Seeker. A founder note from Kay taking the quiz and sharing his own result (the "founder goes first" archetype).
- **X / Nigerian tech Twitter:** the toolkit marks it required for the Self-Made. The original brief excluded it. Flag and recommend.
- **Partners as amplifiers:** turn the 437-handle Instagram coach list, current coaches and community partners into quiz amplifiers, not negotiation targets. Give them a ready kit: their own result card, a co-branded link with tracking, a two-line caption, and a "quiz your clients" angle. No negotiation, just a reason to post.

Give a week-by-week plan with daily seeding volumes that tie back to the 10,000-a-day target.

### 5.9 Measurement
Instrument: landing views, quiz starts, completion rate, teaser-to-capture rate, share rate, forwards per sharer, viral coefficient (K), signup rate, and first circle invite sent. Break down by channel and by archetype.

Flag the two or three numbers that decide whether this works (expect completion rate, share rate and K), set a target and a kill threshold for each, and write one learning hypothesis in the social skill's format: "We believe [posture] will [response] because [insight]. This tests [one variable]."

## 6. QUALITY GATES

Before you deliver, run every member-facing line through the social skill's non-negotiable gates (section 24). Fail and rewrite any line that:
- shames, coddles, or lowers the standard;
- makes a lone achiever the hero with the circle as decoration;
- claims an unverified result, feature or statistic;
- contains an em-dash or any AI-writing tell;
- could run unchanged on a generic fitness or productivity app.

Then score the result copy and the share message on the social skill's 20-point review and report the scores.
