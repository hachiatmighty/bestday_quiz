# Bestday voice for the Life Audit

Copied from the Notion "Bestday Voice Guide" (current standard, adopted 9 November 2026). Where this disagrees with older docs, including the playbook's `voice.md`, this wins.

**Bestday speaks like the one person who remembers your goal, takes it as seriously as you do, and tells you the truth about it in plain words.**

## Who we write for

English-speaking Africans aged 25 to 40, at home and abroad: Lagos, Accra, Nairobi, Johannesburg, London, New York, Toronto. Door open to every Black professional who recognises themselves in them.

- They already have a system (Notion boards, Sunday reviews, quarterly plans).
- They are solo by default, and accept being held because it works.
- They carry other people: family, friends, the group everyone relies on.
- They fear looking "not serious".
- They show wins and hide struggle. Struggle is shared only with a trusted few.
- They are tired of noise.

Test: would the line read the same to a member in Lagos, Nairobi and Brooklyn?

## Personality: the Devoted Ruler, quiet in confidence

| Trait | Sounds like | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm, never just soft | "Three weeks at the gym. Your circle noticed." | "You're amazing! Keep shining!" |
| Exacting, never harsh | "You said Q3. It's September. What's the one thing left?" | "No excuses." |
| Present, never just available | One Monday message that matters | Five pushes a day |
| Grounded, never plain | "Between the deadline and the family group chat, the goal slipped. Pick it back up." | "Unlock your potential." |
| Rousing, never loud | "You already have the system. Now add the people." | "Crush it!" |

One sentence to hold on to: **you already have the system; what's missing is the people.**

## How we write

1. Treat them as capable. Never explain goal-setting, blame willpower, or imply laziness.
2. Lead with a human truth, not a feature.
3. Be specific. Name the goal, the week, the number they gave us.
4. Always put a second person in the sentence: the circle, a friend, "your people".
5. Say it plainly, once.
6. Celebrate in public, hold struggle in private. (For the audit: scores are private by default.)
7. Proof is a person. Never a borrowed statistic or invented testimonial.
8. End with one clear next step, named for what they get: "Start your circle".

Reflective voice (School of Life tradition) suits the result write-up: state the subject plainly, normalise first ("many of us"), turn and reason, leave the decision to them, land one line on its own, invite with the circle named.

## Mechanics

- First line stands alone on a phone. Paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences.
- British spelling: realise, organise, programme.
- No long dashes. Full stop or comma.
- Sentence case for headlines and buttons.
- Numbers as numerals: 3 weeks, 5 people, 3 minutes.
- At most one exclamation mark, only to celebrate.
- No emoji in headlines.

## Words

| Say | Not |
| --- | --- |
| members | users, customers |
| your circle, your people | network, community, group |
| follow through | crush, smash, hack, optimise |
| serious, intentional | motivated, passionate |
| consistent | disciplined |
| witnessed, seen, noticed | tracked, monitored |
| standard, milestone | streak, score, points, badge |

Never say: level up, crush your goals, grind, 10x, supercharge, unlock (your potential), become your best self, hold space, journey, users, the 95% statistic, no excuses, unserious, "It's not just X, it's Y", elevate, game-changer, dream big, stay motivated. No clinical words: assessment (in copy), trauma, self-sabotage, burnout as identity.

Note for the audit: "score" is on the banned list, so the 0 to 10 answers are "ratings" in code and are never called scores in copy. Say "your wheel".

## Culture rules

- Clean international English. No Pidgin, Sheng, AAVE or slang.
- Faith respected, never used. No "blessed", "by grace", "testimony". (The "Meaning and contribution" area may mention faith only as one example among several, chosen by the reader.)
- Rotate place examples across Lagos, Accra, Nairobi, Johannesburg, London, New York, Toronto.
- Never frame carrying others as a weakness.
- Do not promise a human coach.
