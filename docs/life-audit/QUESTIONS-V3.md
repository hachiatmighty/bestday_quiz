# Bestday Life Audit: questions v3

Copy and measurement draft for Q3. No site implementation. [STRUCTURE-V3.md](STRUCTURE-V3.md) governs the sequence and item mix for this task, including about 8 minutes, rather than the older timings and single-item format in SPEC.md. Area names and order remain those in [audit.mjs](../../life-audit/data/audit.mjs). Copy follows [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) and [member-language.md](research/member-language.md); measurement cautions and independent item writing follow [R1 sections C and F](research/R1-instruments.md#c-measurement-design-findings).

Editorial notes, source quotations and the 2 contrast columns are for review only. The member statements, prompts, answer options, role descriptions and result templates are the proposed member-facing copy. Contrasts are newly written examples of register, not quotations from another quiz or a coach. No scale items or close paraphrases were taken from named instruments.

The evidence is 4 cleaned interviews, including a team member and members connected to Bestday. A quote grounds vocabulary or a situation, not prevalence or proof that a new statement measures an area. An inferred outcome is labelled. Creator, Zikoko and Bestday brief lines are not used as member evidence.

## 1. Context: 1 tap

**Prompt:** What has you taking stock right now?

| Option | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Quarter | I am checking how this quarter is going |
| Change | Something has changed |
| Repeat | I keep moving the same thing to next week |
| Curious | Just curious |

Choose 1. No default. Context can introduce their result but does not enter an area reading or choose a role.

## 2. Part 1: where things are

**Intro:** Think about the past 4 weeks. Tap how much each statement sounds like what actually happened.

**Supporting line:** This takes about 8 minutes. You can skip an area.

| Answer label | Internal value |
| --- | ---: |
| Not me | 0 |
| Mostly not me | 1 |
| A bit like me | 2 |
| Mostly me | 3 |
| That's me | 4 |

No preselected answer. One tap advances; Back keeps answers. **Skip this area** remains available throughout each area and its closing check. Skipped areas stay missing. No answer is a judgement of effort or character.

## 3. Parts 1 and 2: the 8 areas

Each table is the first pass of **6 candidates**, including the 2 later cut. The pick beneath it fixes the final **4**, in W1, W2, C1, C2 order: 2 working and 2 cost. W3 and C3 are not additional questions. Word counts cover each entire member statement, including cut candidates. Source numbers refer to the numbered quotes in member-language.md; quotation text and names are preserved exactly, including source spelling and written-out numbers.

The shared closing prompt is **How is it going, in a word?** These are the 5 exact v3 options, repeated below for a complete area handoff. Despite the name, the answers are phrases.
